# AI Baby design direction

- **Audience**: couples and gift-givers curious about an imagined baby portrait, usually on a phone, often sharing the result with a partner or family.
- **Mood**: a calm nursery. Blush pink and sky blue on white, airy spacing, soft shadows; tender without looking like a baby-shower card.
- **Fonts**: headings Nunito 800; body Nunito 400; labels Nunito 700 with wide tracking.
- **Colors**: ink `#37303a`, muted `#8a7d88`, line `#f1dfe8`, accent `#d1769b`, surface `#ffffff`, wash `#fbf3f7`.
- **Texture**: a pale blush-to-blue vertical wash behind the page; the generator is a white card with 32px corners and a soft rose shadow. Buttons are pill-shaped rose. Pricing cards are rounded pastel tiles.
- **Home first screen**: centered rounded headline, two-line lead that states the result is imagined, then the white generator card. Results use the same soft card; the footnote repeats that it is not a prediction.
- **Avoid**: hospital or clinical blues, dark backgrounds, sharp corners, and the cream gallery look of the pet site.
