# Ghibli Photo design direction

- **Audience**: people who want a soft, hand-painted version of a travel photo, pet or street scene to share, usually arriving from a search for a specific look.
- **Mood**: a quiet illustrated postcard. Sky-blue haze over meadow green, rounded storybook edges, warm and hand-made rather than techy.
- **Fonts**: headings Lora; body Nunito; small labels Nunito with generous letter spacing.
- **Colors**: ink `#22312a`, muted `#5f7268`, line `#cfe0d2`, accent `#3f7d5a`, surface `#fcfdf8`, wash `#edf5ec`.
- **Texture**: a fixed vertical sky-to-meadow gradient behind the page; the generator is a large soft card with 28px corners and a pale green outline. Buttons are pill-shaped forest green. Pricing cards look like postcards.
- **Home first screen**: centered Lora headline, short lead, then the rounded generator card. Results sit in the same rounded frame; a footnote reminds visitors the scene is imagined.
- **Avoid**: dark backgrounds, neon greens, flat white dashboards, sharp corners, and the pet site's cream-and-terracotta palette.
