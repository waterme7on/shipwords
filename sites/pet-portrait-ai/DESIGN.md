# Pet Portrait AI design direction

- **Audience**: pet owners who want a giftable, gallery-style portrait of their dog or cat, usually shopping from a phone after taking a photo.
- **Mood**: a small warm gallery. Cream paper, dark umber ink and one terracotta accent; quiet, expensive, never cartoonish.
- **Fonts**: headings Fraunces (optical serif); body Inter; small labels and prices Inter with tight tracking.
- **Colors**: ink `#2b1d16`, muted `#7a6252`, line `#e4d3c0`, accent `#a94f2a`, surface `#fffaf3`, wash `#f4eadc`.
- **Texture**: a soft radial wash behind the page; the generator sits on a cream card with a thin frame shadow, like a matted print. Buttons are solid terracotta with square-ish 8px corners.
- **Home first screen**: centered serif headline, one-line lead, then the framed generator card. Results take the same frame treatment; pricing reads as gallery price labels.
- **Avoid**: blue SaaS gradients, rounded blob shapes, neon accents, dark mode, and the template's light-grey look.
